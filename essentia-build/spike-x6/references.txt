=== Known reference-VERSION effects (8.x vs 7.1.1) - NOT loader regressions
Measured: the .5 loader (build A) and the .6 loader are each bit-exact against
7.1.1 on every row below, and both show the same differences against 8.x.
  - AAC in MP4 (m4a): 8.x outputs 288-867 FEWER sample frames than 7.1.1 - it
    trims the encoder's end padding; 7.1.1 does not. The overlap is
    bit-identical (aac_44k_st -655, aac_48k_mo -288, aac_96k_st -576,
    aac_44k_st_short -867). Real m4a rows in this corpus have equal counts.
  - AAC (incl. raw ADTS) and WMA (wmav2) float PCM: last-bit differences,
    max |d| 1.9e-9 .. 1.2e-7, equal counts (decoder arithmetic changed
    between libavcodec 61 and 63).
  - Every other codec in the corpus (FLAC, PCM WAV/AIFF, MP3, Vorbis, ALAC,
    WavPack): bit-identical between the two references.
Source: parity-build-a.txt / parity-x6.txt (gate) vs parity-build-a-shipped.txt /
parity-x6-shipped.txt.

=== FFmpeg limit found while building the corpus (both versions; not a loader defect)
FFmpeg's flac encoder writes 24-bit streams with block size 65535 that NO FFmpeg
(7.1.1 or 8.x, ffmpeg or ffprobe) decodes a sample of, when the content barely
compresses (seeded noise, or a tone at 0.4 x the rate; seen at 96k and 192k).
The corpus' 24-bit big-block row is therefore generated from two low tones only.
